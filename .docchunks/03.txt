## Discover then Replay

Discover live:
npm run discover -- --goal "look up member 10001 and read their current savings balance" --url http://127.0.0.1:4173 --param memberId=10001

Discover without key:
npm run discover -- --goal "look up member and read savings balance" --url http://127.0.0.1:4173 --param memberId=10001 --synthetic-fallback
